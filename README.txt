Currently in BETA testing! All builds may have bugs and are subject to change as more features are developed.

The Herbalist mod is a simple mod that adds a few medicinal plants and their consumption to Minecraft!

This mod adds the following features:

- 11 New plants, herbs, and medicines
- A new music disc
- A variety of new effects
- Compatibility with Tobacconist mod

Check out the Tobacconist mod for additional compatibility and all your tobacco needs: Tobacconist - Mods - Minecraft - CurseForge
